# 5-Minute Live Interview Demonstration Script
## Project: ORACLE — Autonomous Decision Intelligence Engine

---

### Minute 1: The Strategic Problem & Executive Scorecard (0:00 - 1:00)
- **What to say**:
  > *"Leadership teams at high-growth companies face data latency. A CFO or VP often waits days for a data team to write SQL reports just to understand why a budget was exceeded. I built ORACLE to function as an autonomous senior business analyst that translates plain-English questions into safe read-only SQL, conducts multi-step investigations, and estimates the financial impact of strategic interventions."*
- **What to show**:
  - Show the **Executive Overview** page.
  - Point to the 4 key metrics: **ARR ($10.24M)**, **Gross Margin (74.5%)**, **Operating Cashflow ($1.7M)**, and **Churn Risk Exposure ($284.5K)**.

---

### Minute 2: Trailing P&L Curve & Automated Anomaly Radar (1:00 - 2:00)
- **What to say**:
  > *"Looking at the 12-month P&L visualizer, we immediately notice operating margin compression in Q3. Our automated anomaly radar flagged that Growth & Performance Marketing exceeded its quarterly budget by $92,400 due to unconstrained paid acquisition channels."*
- **What to show**:
  - Point out the Q3 bar in the P&L chart highlighting the OPEX expansion.
  - Review the **Automated Anomaly Radar** cards on the right.

---

### Minute 3: Natural Language to SQL Execution (2:00 - 3:00)
- **What to say**:
  > *"Instead of logging a ticket with data engineering, an executive simply asks ORACLE directly. Let's ask: 'What are our top most profitable products?'"*
- **What to show**:
  - Click the **AI Query Engine** tab.
  - Click the prompt pill or type the query, and click **Analyze & Execute SQL**.
  - Show the executed SQL query: `SELECT name, category, unit_price, margin_percent...`.
  - Highlight the read-only sandbox badge ensuring database safety.
  - Review the plain-English executive synthesis, key insights, and actionable recommendation.

---

### Minute 4: Deep-Dive Investigation & Financial Impact (3:00 - 4:00)
- **What to say**:
  > *"When an issue requires deeper investigation, ORACLE initiates an automated investigation case. Let's inspect Case INV-2026-001 regarding the Marketing budget overrun."*
- **What to show**:
  - Switch to the **Investigations** tab.
  - Review the hypothesis, core analytical findings (Customer Acquisition Cost rose to $540), and the prescribed intervention.
  - Point out the **+$145,000 USD** projected impact.

---

### Minute 5: Human-in-the-Loop Governance & Briefing Export (4:00 - 5:00)
- **What to say**:
  > *"Because consequential business decisions should never be executed blindly by AI, ORACLE enforces strict human-in-the-loop governance. An executive must review the evidence and authorize the intervention."*
- **What to show**:
  - Click **"Authorize Intervention"** on the case. Watch the status transition to `RESOLVED / AUTHORIZED`.
  - Switch to the **Executive Briefing** tab to display the print-ready executive dossier.
